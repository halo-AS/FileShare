Persona - A technologically proficient young man or woman focused on efficiency. 

Scenario - This young man or woman wants to quickly share files between their mobile device
and their computer. However, there exist no means of quick and smooth file sharing between
their devices. All services require you to get a link in order to access the uploaded file,
or they require you to spend time creating an account to access the site's services. This
creates frustration, and takes up their highly valued time.

Our solution - A simple website that requires no user sign-ups, and provides the user a
simple pairing code to download their file. This eliminates the time spent setting up an 
account, and removes the frustration of finding a way to send the download link from one
device to another.
